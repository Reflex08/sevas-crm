Privacy Policy

Seva's Services (window and gutter cleaning, Toronto) — effective August 2026.

What we collect

When you request a quote through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, our website, a referral link, or by contacting us directly, we receive the details you submit: name, phone number, email, service address, and your answers to the form questions.

How we use it

Only to respond to your request, provide a quote, schedule and perform the service, send booking confirmations and reminders, and follow up about the work we did for you. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their marketing.

Where it lives

Your details are stored in our private customer-management system, accessible only to Seva's Services staff. Lead-form submissions pass through a private relay hosted on Cloudflare and are retained there for at most 30 days.

Facebook / Meta data

Lead-form data received from Meta Platforms is used solely to contact you about the service you asked about. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

Text or email us and we will correct or delete your information. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out of further messages.
